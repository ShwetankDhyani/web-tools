About WebTools.wiki

A small, open toolkit for jobs you do every week online.

WebTools.wiki ships three focused tools:

  • Video Downloader — paste a link, pick quality, save the file.
  • Paywall Remover — read articles in a clean reader view.
  • Price Tracker — Telegram alerts when a product hits your target.

Install on your phone: open this site in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone), then Add to Home Screen / Install. After that, use your phone’s Share menu on any link and pick WebTools to send it into Video, Paywall, or Price Tracker.

No ads. No premium tiers. Code is on GitHub so you can inspect, self-host, or contribute.
