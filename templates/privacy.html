Privacy

What we collect, why, and what we never do.

Short version

Video Downloader and Paywall Remover do not require an account. Price Tracker signs you in with a Telegram username so we can send price alerts. We do not sell personal data or show ads.

What we process

  • Video / Paywall: the URL you submit, processed server-side to fetch content. We do not create accounts for these tools.
  • Price Tracker: Telegram username, product URLs, target prices, and price history needed to alert you.
  • Logs: standard server logs (IP, timestamps, errors) for reliability and abuse prevention.

Third parties

Price Tracker login and alerts use CallMeBot to deliver Telegram messages. Video metadata/downloads use yt-dlp against the sites you request. Article fetching may use public archives when a publisher blocks direct access.

Retention

Tracked products and related history stay until you remove them (or an admin cleans notified/erroring rows). Temporary video download files are deleted after a short TTL. OTP codes expire in minutes.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on GitHub.
